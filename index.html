About

Engineer by training.
Infrastructure by obsession.

My foundation is Mechatronics — a discipline that forces you to think in systems. Mechanical, electrical, software — all interacting, all failing in different ways. That training shaped how I approach infrastructure: not as a collection of tools, but as a system with failure modes to anticipate and contain.

I moved into cloud infrastructure because that is where systems thinking has the most leverage. I design platforms where failure is expected, isolated, and recovered from automatically — not platforms that pretend failure won't happen.

Today I work at the intersection of reliability engineering, platform automation, and cloud cost visibility. My goal is the same every time — build platforms that are boring to operate because they handle the hard parts themselves.
